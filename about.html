OUR STORY

Born from a love of great pizza.

Fire & Slice started with one simple idea: pizza should feel special without feeling fancy. We make our dough from scratch, use quality ingredients, and cook every pie hot and fast.

From the first crackle of the crust to the last stretch of mozzarella, we want every visit to feel like your favorite neighborhood tradition.

What We Believe

Craft

Slow dough, careful toppings and a blistering oven make the difference.

Quality

We choose ingredients for flavor first, not shortcuts.

Community

A great pizza table is better when everyone has a seat.
